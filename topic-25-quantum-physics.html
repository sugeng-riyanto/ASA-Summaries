Quantum Physics

E = hfE = hc/λ1 eV = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ Jp = E/chf = Φ + ½mv²λ = h/phf = E₁ − E₂
EXAMINER'S BRIEF

The single idea to absorb: light interacts with matter as particles (photons) but travels as waves. The photoelectric effect is the particle evidence, interference/diffraction the wave evidence — and electrons show the mirror image. Every calculation lives on two equations: E = hf and hf = Φ + ½mv²_max.

1 · The Photon

Max Planck (1901) suggested energy comes in discrete packets — quanta. Einstein (1905) applied the idea to light: a photon is a quantum of electromagnetic energy. All EM radiation is made of photons; a γ-photon is the most energetic, a radio photon the least.

EINSTEIN RELATION

E = hf = hc/λ

E = photon energy (J) · f = frequency (Hz) · λ = wavelength (m)

h = Planck constant = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s. Photon energy is proportional to frequency, hence inversely proportional to wavelength — a short-wavelength X-ray photon packs far more punch than red light.

THE BRIDGE

E = hf ties a particle property (energy E) to a wave property (frequency f) — the first hint of duality. c = fλ connects the two forms.

WORKED · LED

An LED emits 670 nm light at 50 mW. Photon energy E = hc/λ = (6.63×10⁻³⁴ × 3.0×10⁸)/(670×10⁻⁹) = 2.97×10⁻¹⁹ J. Rate = power/energy = 50×10⁻³ / 2.97×10⁻¹⁹ ≈ 1.7 × 10¹⁷ photons s⁻¹.

2 · The Electronvolt

DEFINITION

The electronvolt is the energy gained by an electron travelling through a potential difference of one volt.

1 eV = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ J

from W = QV = 1.60×10⁻¹⁹ × 1

CONVERTING

J → eV: divide by 1.6×10⁻¹⁹.  eV → J: multiply by 1.6×10⁻¹⁹. Joules are clumsy for photon energies — e.g. visible photons are ~2 eV, not 3×10⁻¹⁹ J.

ACCELERATION

A particle of charge e accelerated from rest through p.d. V gains kinetic energy eV (the equation works below relativistic speeds): ½mv² = eV. A proton through 1500 V gains 1500 eV = 2.4×10⁻¹⁶ J.

3 · Photon Momentum

A photon has momentum even though it has no mass — Einstein showed p = E/c from relativity. Sunlight raining photons exerts tiny pressure on objects in space (comet tails blow away from the Sun for this reason).

PHOTON MOMENTUM

p = E/c = hf/c = h/λ

units kg m s⁻¹ (or N s)

DE BROGLIE'S ECHO

p = h/λ is exactly the de Broglie equation you will meet for particles — one equation spans both worlds: photon and electron.

4 · The Photoelectric Effect

THE EXPERIMENT

Clean zinc plate on a charged gold-leaf electroscope. Ultraviolet from a mercury lamp makes the leaf fall (electrons escape, charge leaks). Visible light — any brightness — does nothing. A sheet of glass (which blocks UV) stops the effect too.

KEY TERMS

Photoelectric emission: release of electrons from a metal surface when EM radiation shines on it. The emitted electrons are photoelectrons.

Threshold frequency f₀: minimum frequency of radiation that releases electrons.

Threshold wavelength λ₀: the longest wavelength that still releases electrons (λ₀ = c/f₀).

Work function Φ: minimum energy needed for an electron to escape the surface.

WHY WAVE THEORY FAILS
  • Wave theory says brighter light → more energy → emission; but below f₀ no intensity, however huge, works.
  • Wave theory predicts higher intensity → faster electrons; but intensity changes only the number of electrons, never their maximum kinetic energy.
  • Wave theory predicts energy accumulation with time; emission is actually instantaneous.

Only the photon model explains all three.

5 · Einstein's Equation & Thresholds

PHOTOELECTRIC EQUATION

One photon interacts with one electron and transfers all its energy instantly. Conservation of energy:

hf = Φ + ½mv²_max

photon energy = work function + max kinetic energy of the photoelectron

① Photon of energy hf arrives at the metal.
▼
② A single electron absorbs the whole packet.
▼
③ Φ is spent escaping the energy well; the rest is kinetic energy.
AT THRESHOLD

At f = f₀ the electron just escapes with zero kinetic energy:

hf₀ = Φ    so    f₀ = Φ/h

Electrons deeper in the well lose more to collisions and escape with less than the maximum KE — hence a range of kinetic energies up to ½mv²_max.

THE h GRAPH

Plotting max KE against frequency gives a straight line with gradient = h and intercept on the KE axis = −Φ; it cuts the frequency axis at f₀. A favourite exam question.

6 · Intensity vs Frequency

Quantity you changeWhat happens
Intensity ↑ (same frequency, above threshold)Photoelectric current ↑ proportionally — more photons per second ⇒ more photoelectrons per second.
Intensity ↑No change to the maximum kinetic energy — each photon still carries the same hf.
Frequency ↑ (above threshold)Maximum kinetic energy ↑ — each photon is more energetic (line slope = h).
Frequency below f₀No emission at all — each photon alone is too weak, whatever the intensity.
TWO DIFFERENT "INTENSITY" FACTS

Current ∝ intensity but KE is independent of intensity. Confuse these and you lose the easiest marks on the paper. Rate of photon arrival is what intensity controls; per-photon energy is what frequency controls.

7 · Wave–Particle Duality

EVIDENCE, SPLIT IN TWO
  • Wave nature of light: interference, diffraction, polarisation (Young's slits, gratings).
  • Particle nature of light: photoelectric effect, Geiger-counter clicks from individual γ-photons.
THE RULE OF THUMB

Interaction ⇒ particles; propagation ⇒ waves. Light is absorbed/emitted in photon lumps, but spreads through space as a wave. Both models are needed — the choice depends on what you are explaining.

8 · de Broglie & Electron Diffraction

Louis de Broglie (1924) argued: if waves can behave as particles, particles should behave as waves. Every moving particle carries an associated wavelength — the de Broglie wavelength.

DE BROGLIE EQUATION

λ = h/p = h/mv

λ = de Broglie wavelength (m) · p = momentum (kg m s⁻¹) · m = mass · v = speed

ELECTRON DIFFRACTION TUBE

Electrons accelerated by a high anode–cathode voltage pass through a thin film of polycrystalline graphite; the atomic layers act like a diffraction grating and rings appear on the phosphor screen. Raising the voltage shrinks the rings — faster electrons have shorter wavelengths, and diffraction depends on λ matching the atomic spacing (~10⁻¹⁰ m).

THE DUAL ELECTRON

Particle evidence: each electron lands as a single flash, Newtonian mechanics works. Wave evidence: the flashes build up into a diffraction pattern — which individual particles could never produce.

WHY WE DON'T DIFFRACT

λ = h/mv with a person's mass is ~10⁻³⁶ m — utterly invisible next to any doorway. The wave model matters only when λ is comparable to the gap: electrons, neutrons (~10⁻¹⁰ m) probing crystals, or high-energy electrons (~10⁻¹⁵ m) probing nuclei.

9 · Energy Levels & Line Spectra

TWO SPECTRA
  • Continuous: hot solids/liquids/dense gases — all wavelengths (white light, 400–700 nm).
  • Line: isolated atoms (low-pressure gas, discharge tube) — only specific wavelengths, unique to each element. Hot-gas emission = emission line spectrum; white light through cool gas = absorption line spectrum (dark lines on a continuous background — the Sun's Fraunhofer lines).
QUANTISED ENERGY

An electron in an isolated atom may have only certain energies — the energy levels. Energies are negative because the electron is trapped by the nucleus; zero means free (ionised).

hf = E₁ − E₂

transition down ⇒ photon emitted · transition up ⇒ photon absorbed (exact match required)

① Electron absorbs a photon of exactly the right energy and jumps up — excited state.
▼
② The excited atom is unstable; the electron falls back down.
▼
③ One photon of energy hf = E₁ − E₂ is emitted in a random direction.
BIGGER JUMP, SHORTER WAVELENGTH

The larger the energy gap, the higher the frequency and the shorter the wavelength. Absorption lines appear dark because re-emitted photons scatter in all directions, so less light continues straight towards you.

10 · Worked Examples

PHOTOELECTRIC

Zinc, Φ = 6.9×10⁻¹⁹ J. Threshold: f₀ = Φ/h = 6.9×10⁻¹⁹/6.63×10⁻³⁴ = 1.04×10¹⁵ Hz. For UV of f = 1.5×10¹⁵ Hz: ½mv²_max = hf − Φ = 6.63×10⁻³⁴(1.5×10¹⁵ − 1.04×10¹⁵) = 3.1×10⁻¹⁹ J.

DE BROGLIE

Electron at 1.0×10⁷ m s⁻¹: λ = h/mv = 6.63×10⁻³⁴/(9.11×10⁻³¹ × 1.0×10⁷) = 7.3×10⁻¹¹ m — comparable to atomic spacing (~10⁻¹⁰ m), so the electron is diffracted by matter.

ENERGY LEVEL

Hydrogen transition from −0.54×10⁻¹⁸ J to −2.18×10⁻¹⁸ J: ΔE = 1.64×10⁻¹⁸ J → λ = hc/ΔE = (6.63×10⁻³⁴ × 3.0×10⁸)/1.64×10⁻¹⁸ = 1.21×10⁻⁷ m = 121 nm (ultraviolet).

IONISATION

Hydrogen ground state −13.6 eV: ionisation energy = 13.6 eV = 2.18×10⁻¹⁸ J → λ = hc/ΔE ≈ 91 nm.

TRAP LIST
  • ✗ mixing up eV and J — convert via 1 eV = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ J
  • ✗ "higher intensity ⇒ faster electrons" — intensity only raises the current
  • ✗ saying light "sometimes is a particle" — it's both, by circumstance
  • ✗ using λ = h/mv for a photon (massless!) — use p = E/c
  • ✗ absorption line = photon re-emitted forward — it's scattered sideways
