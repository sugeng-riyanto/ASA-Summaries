9 · Trigonometric & Useful Maths

TRIG
  • sin θ = opp/hyp, cos θ = adj/hyp, tan = opp/adj
  • sin(90°−θ) = cos θ
  • small θ: sin θ ≈ θ, tan θ ≈ θ (radians)
  • sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
CALC IN EXAMS
  • Exponential: use ln for y = a·e^(bx)
  • Logs base10: lg (P/Q) = lg P − lg Q
  • Percentage diff = |diff|/mean × 100
  • Straight line: y = mx + c
  • Uncertainties combine: first rule for +/−

10 · Exam Technique Notes

DATA INVERSIONS & RULES
  • Use the data only where physics demands — sensibly rounded.
  • Always quote answers to a sensible precision (usually 2–3 sf unless given).
  • When asked "define", quote the formula definition: x = … with symbols defined.
  • "State and explain" → pair the equation with the physics reason.
  • Give units unless dimensionless.
