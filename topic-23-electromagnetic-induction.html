Electromagnetic Induction

Φ = BAflux linkage = NΦE = −d(NΦ)/dtE = BLvFaradayLenz
EXAMINER'S BRIEF

This is the payoff chapter — it explains how all electricity is generated. The engine is Faraday's law: induced e.m.f. = rate of change of flux linkage, and the conscience of it all is Lenz's law: the induced effect always opposes the change producing it (that minus sign IS conservation of energy). Master the flux-linkage-versus-time graph — e.m.f. is its −gradient — and you can handle every rotating-coil question.

1 · Observing Induction

FOUR EXPERIMENTS, ONE IDEA
  • Experiment 1 — spin a small motor by hand: connected to a voltmeter it becomes a generator. Spin faster → bigger deflection. Off → zero.
  • Experiment 2 — move a bar magnet in/out of a coil on a microammeter: deflection each time it moves; none when still; bigger for faster moves and stronger magnets; reverses for the other pole.
  • Experiment 3 — move a wire through the field between poles: microammeter kicks. Double the length in the field → double the deflection. Make a loop and both sides cut → bigger again.
WHAT THE SIZE DEPENDS ON

Straight wire: B, length L in the field, and the wire's speed. Coil: B, area, angle of plane to field, number of turns, and the rate the coil turns.

2 · Cutting Field Lines

THE PICTURE

When a conductor moves through a magnetic field it cuts the field lines. Cutting lines in either direction − conductor moved, or magnet moved past the conductor − is what creates induced e.m.f. It does not matter which one moves; only their relative motion matters.

NOT A COMPLETE CIRCUIT?

Then no current flows — instead charge piles up, giving a p.d. induced e.m.f. proper. It is truly an e.m.f.: connect the ends to a lamp and electrons flow, so it supplies energy. In the conductor, conventional current runs negative to positive, exactly like inside a cell.

WHY ELECTRONS MOVE

Moving the wire gives free electrons a velocity; each electron moving through a B field feels a Bev force sideways. That push accumulates electrons at one end — the driven "source".

3 · Magnetic Flux — Φ = BA

DEFINITION

Magnetic flux through area A = the product of the component of B perpendicular to the area, and A.

Φ = BA   (B normal to area)

at angle θ to the normal: Φ = BA cos θ

θ = 90° (B in plane) → Φ = 0; θ = 0° (normal) → Φ = BA

Unit: weber (Wb) = T m². Picture it as the total number of field lines through the area.

WORKED

B = 2.0×10⁻³ T, solenoid A = 0.10 m², θ = 0°, N = 250: Φ = BA = 2.0×10⁻³ × 0.10 = 2.0×10⁻⁴ Wb.

4 · Flux Linkage — NΦ

COILS

If the same flux threads every one of N turns, the total is:

flux linkage = NΦ = BAN

also in weber (Wb) · 1 Wb = 1 T m²

THREE WAYS TO INDUCE

Since Φ depends on B, A and θ, an e.m.f. comes from changing any of: B (move a magnet), A (squash the loop), or θ (rotate the coil in the field).

WORKED

Solenoid A = 0.10 m², B = 2.0×10⁻³ T, N = 250: linkage = NΦ = 250 × 2.0×10⁻⁴ = 5.0×10⁻² Wb.

5 · Faraday's Law

MAGNITUDE

The magnitude of the induced e.m.f. equals the rate of change of flux linkage:

E = Δ(NΦ)/Δt = d(NΦ)/dt

proportional quantity equals SI 1.

SEARCH COIL

Many turns of fine wire, pulled out of a field → measures E and hence B. Worked: N = 2500, A = 1.2×10⁻⁴ m², B = 0.50 T, out in 0.10 s:
Δlinkage = 0 − (2500 × 1.2×10⁻⁴ × 0.50) = −0.15 Wb, E = 0.15/0.10 = 1.5 V (average).

6 · E = BLv (Moving Wire)

ONE-CONDUCTOR CASE

Wire length L moving at speed v at right angles to B. In time t it sweeps area L·(vt), so:

ΔΦ = B × (L vt) → E = BLv

v ⊥ B ⊥ L for this simple form

WORKED

L = 0.20 m, v = 3.0 m s⁻¹, B = 0.10 T → E = 0.10 × 3.0 × 0.20 = 0.060 V = 60 mV.
Aircraft wingspan L, flying at v in vertical B: same E = BLv — the "wingtip e.m.f."

7 · Lenz's Law & Conservation of Energy

ENERGY BEFORE SECURITY

Think about pushing north pole into a coil. If the induced current made the nearby end a south pole, the magnet would be sucked in for free — kinetic + electrical energy out of nothing! That violates conservation of energy.

So the induced current must make the near end a north pole — it repels the approaching magnet. You must push against it; your work becomes the electrical energy.

LENZ'S LAW

Any induced e.m. is established in a direction so as to produce effects that oppose the change producing it.

E = −d(NΦ)/dt

the minus sign is Lenz — the direction of the e.m.f. always opposes its own cause

WHY THE SPRING-DOWN DYNAMO MYSTERY

Turning a dynamo with lamps on is harder than lights-off: when current flows, Lenz's rule gives a braking force on the rotor. That extra effort = the electrical energy you're spending. (Energy is never free.)

8 · Direction — Fleming's Right-Hand (Generator) Rule

RIGHT-HAND RULE

First finger = Field · seCond finger = Current (induced) · thuMb = Motion

Same three letters as the left-hand rule, but this is the "generator" mirror: motion causes the current.

MAGNET & COIL SENSE

A north pole approaching a coil → the coil end nearest acts as a north pole (repels). Pulling a north pole out → coil end becomes a south pole (attracts, opposing the removal). In every case the induced field fights the change.

9 · Generators & the A.C. Waveform

SPIN THE COIL

Rotate a coil (or keep it fixed and spin an electromagnet inside) and the emf swings sinusoidally. The induced emf is the gradient of the flux-linkage graph:

E = −d(NΦ)/dt GRAPH

when flux is a maximum → gradient zero → e.m.f. zero
when flux passes through zero → gradient steepest → e.m.f. maximum

E-peak = BAN · 2πf (NФ maximum × angular frequency)

WHY MAX EMF AT ZERO FLUX

Imagine the coil at the "edge-on" moment: flux threading is zero but an instant later a lot of it pours through — the rate of change is huge. e.m.f. ∝ slope, not level.

10 · Transformers & Exam Skills

TRANSFORMER

An alternating current in the primary creates a changing flux in a soft-iron core. The same changing flux links the secondary → induced e.m.f. there.

V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p

no changing flux → no induced emf — so a d.c. supply gives nothing

FORMULA BANK

Φ = BA cos θ · linkage = NΦ = BAN
E = d(NΦ)/dt (Faraday)
minus sign Lenz's law
E = BLv (moving wire)
E_peak = BAN(2πf) for a rotating coil
1 Wb = 1 Tm²

TRAP LIST
  • ✗ saying d.c. transformer "produces" — a steady current makes steady flux, no change → nothing
  • ✗ mixing flux (BA) and flux linkage (NΦ) — they have the same units but differ by N
  • ✗ Sketching e.m.f vs time without the 90° phase shift (e.m.f. max when flux zero)
  • ✗ dropping the minus sign in explanations — Lenz is energy itself

Cut lines, feel the minus: flux resists its own change. ✔
